PHOTO POLICY

- Homepage and category photography uses unique Unsplash images.
- No category image is reused for another category.
- Recipe cards request the original source page's preview image.
- If a recipe has no usable source preview, the card displays an elegant
  navy no-photo treatment rather than repeating an unrelated food photo.
- Adobe Stock images were not embedded because they require an active license
  through the site owner's Adobe account.
